Privacy Policy — Colors of Summer 3D

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in Colors of Summer 3D, an Android game developed under the author brand Michael Haven. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the developer using that brand.

The game is designed for children approximately 4–8 years old. It can be played offline, without an account. Parents and guardians should handle purchases and support correspondence. An offline copy of this policy is available in the game's Parents section while play is paused.

For privacy questions, contact michaelhaven.games@proton.me.

2. Personal Information

The game does not ask players to register or provide their name, date of birth, email address, postal address or telephone number. It does not access contacts, the camera, the microphone or real-world location. It does not use advertising identifiers or an in-game account identifier.

The game may process technical information locally, as explained below. It does not automatically send gameplay information or diagnostic data to us. We do not use this information to track players across apps or websites, build advertising profiles or sell personal information.

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you include. We use that information to answer your request, investigate the issue you report and handle related follow-up. Please do not include a child's name, photograph, address, school, date of birth or other unnecessary personal information.

3. Local Technical Diagnostics

While the game runs, it may generate technical measurements locally to support gameplay, loading and performance. Depending on the device and runtime state, these measurements may include:

  • Device and software information, such as the WebView user-agent string, which may reveal the phone model and Android or WebView version; graphics hardware information; approximate device memory and available processor-thread count.
  • Display information, including screen and canvas dimensions, device pixel ratio and the selected graphics mode.
  • Performance information, including frame rates, frame and callback timings, drawing counters, loading checkpoints and interruptions caused by pausing, hiding or resizing the game.
  • Game information, including collected seeds, magic charges, completed adventures, the heroine's position inside the game, dress and tree stages, and audio playback status. In-game coordinates are not GPS location.
  • Timestamps, game and diagnostic version information, local page and resource addresses, technical query parameters, and JavaScript or WebGL error messages and stack traces.

These on-device measurements are not designed to include names, contact details, payment credentials, recordings or real-world location. Device details, timestamps and error text may nevertheless be identifying if a person separately chooses to share them with support. Local processing does not by itself send this information to the developer.

The Google Play release does not provide a user-facing diagnostic panel or a built-in JSON diagnostic-report export control. These controls were used in testing builds and are not part of the released game's interface. Technical measurements are not automatically transmitted to the developer.

4. Purchases

Purchases of the game through Google Play are one-time purchases. The game contains no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Google Play handles payment processing and any refunds available under its policies. The game does not ask for, store or transmit your payment-card numbers or banking credentials. Google handles purchase information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Google may make transaction and financial records available to the developer for purchase support, accounting and legal obligations. These records are separate from the game's local measurements. If you contact us about a purchase, provide only the order reference and information needed to address your request; do not send payment-card details or passwords.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The game contains no advertising, advertising SDKs, integrated analytics or automatic crash-reporting services. It does not provide social features, player accounts or chat.

The game uses Android System WebView and bundled software libraries to display and run its local content. It does not contact a developer-operated server during play. The local https://localhost address used inside the Android app refers to files packaged in the app, not to an Internet server.

Google Play, Android system services and any storage or email provider you choose operate separately from the offline game. Support emails are handled through Proton Mail. These providers process information needed to deliver their services under their own policies. We do not send identifiable support messages or attached files to an additional analysis service without first explaining that use to you.

If you read the online copy of this policy on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect that page. GitHub's handling of that information is described in its privacy statement. This is separate from playing the game offline. Our policy page does not include advertising, analytics scripts, third-party fonts or tracking widgets.

6. Data Storage and Deletion

Game state, language preference, audio settings and technical measurements are processed or stored locally on the device; they are not uploaded or synchronized to us. The game uses local storage for preferences and information needed to move between levels. It can also read an existing local reduced-motion preference. It does not provide an online account or a cloud save. A new app launch starts a new game rather than restoring completed gameplay.

You can remove app-managed local data using Android's Clear storage or uninstall options. Emails and any files you choose to send separately to support are held outside the game. Clearing or uninstalling the game does not delete those emails or attachments.

If you voluntarily send us a support message or an attachment of your own choosing, we keep only what is needed to handle the request and related follow-up. After the request is resolved, we review the correspondence and remove attachments and other information that are no longer needed. We do not keep submitted diagnostic attachments indefinitely simply for possible future use. Information that must be retained for an unresolved dispute or a legal or accounting obligation is kept only for that purpose.

To request access to, correction of or deletion of information you sent us, email michaelhaven.games@proton.me. Include enough information to locate the correspondence, such as the sending email address and approximate date; do not send unnecessary information about a child. We will review the request, delete information under our control when it is no longer needed, and explain any applicable retention requirement. Provider-controlled backups may follow the provider's own deletion process.

We cannot directly delete files from your device, your personal cloud storage or records controlled by Google Play. We can explain which provider or device controls apply.

7. Children's Privacy

Protecting children's privacy is central to the game's design. Children can play without providing personal information, creating an account, viewing advertisements or communicating with other players. Local diagnostics are not used for advertising or profiling children.

Purchases and support correspondence should be managed by a parent or guardian. We do not ask children to send personal information. If you believe a child has sent us unnecessary personal information, contact michaelhaven.games@proton.me so that we can locate it, review the circumstances and remove it unless a legal requirement prevents deletion.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the game, support practices or applicable requirements. We will publish updates at the same privacy-policy address and show the actual last-updated date. Material changes will be explained in the updated policy. A policy update alone does not grant access to device permissions or authorize new data collection that requires a separate permission or consent.
